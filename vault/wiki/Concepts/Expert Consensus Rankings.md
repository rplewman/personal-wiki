# Expert Consensus Rankings

> Concept page assembled by `wiki ingest` from facts local Gemma found in each source. Mentioned in 1 source.

## In [[Draft Copilot]]
Recommendations are derived from expert consensus rankings rather than Sleeper's standard ADP.

- Recommendations should come from expert consensus rankings, not Sleeper's ADP. ([[raw/Draft Copilot#Objective|§ Objective]])
- The default rankings blend FantasyPros consensus and ESPN Field Yates PPR rankings. ([[raw/Draft Copilot#Rankings dataset|§ Rankings dataset]])
- A player on both lists gets FantasyPros_rank × 0.6 + ESPN_rank × 0.4, and the list is re-sorted by that blended score. ([[raw/Draft Copilot#Rankings dataset|§ Rankings dataset]])
