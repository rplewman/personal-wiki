## Summary
Breakaway is an intelligent data aggregator and plan adjuster designed to be the intelligent layer between an athlete's wearable data and their coaching plan. Its main goal is to make every serious amateur's training as adaptive and race-specific as if they had a professional coach reviewing their data every morning.
